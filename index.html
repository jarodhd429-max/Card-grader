How this works

Upload front and back photos, cropped tight to the card's physical edges. Drag the four corner handles onto the printed border to measure centering directly from the image. Corner and edge wear are auto-sampled from the photo as a starting point, or tap "Grade with AI" to have Claude look at the photo directly — adjust the sliders yourself if you disagree with either. This is a desk estimate, not a submission-grade guarantee.

Estimates come from your photos, a general price-scaling curve, and — when you tap "Grade with AI" — Claude's own visual read of corners, edges and surface. Lighting, image resolution, photo angle and Claude's read of a single photo all affect accuracy. Actual grades from PSA, BGS, SGC or CGC and actual sale prices can differ meaningfully.
